DiveLog

Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 17, 2026 · Last updated: September 15, 2026

Privacy-first by design. All your dive logs stay on your device. We do not collect, transmit, store on our servers, sync, share, or sell your information. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

Who we are

DiveLog ("the app", "we") is provided by Softgrove, an independent developer based in Japan. Contact: softgrove@duck.com.

What data the app handles

You may record dives — site name, location, dive type, max depth, bottom time, water temperature, visibility, gas mix and O₂ percentage, tank pressures, weight, suit, buddy, rating, and notes ("your dive logs"). Your dive logs are stored only locally on your device using Apple's on-device database, protected with iOS file protection. We never receive this data.

We do not collect: name, email, account credentials (the app has no account), location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics.

Export and deletion

  • Export: You can export your dive logs as a file, or generate a PDF logbook, from within the app. Once you export and share a file, that copy is under your control and outside the app.
  • Delete: You can permanently delete all dive logs at any time from Settings → Delete All Data. Deleting the app also removes its on-device data.

Subscriptions and payments

Premium subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information. Apple's handling of your purchase is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. No notification data is sent to us or any third party.

Children

DiveLog is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region) and collects no information from anyone.

Third parties

The app uses only Apple system frameworks. There are no third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising networks, or developer-operated external servers. Apple’s StoreKit systems process optional purchases.

For users in the EU/EEA and UK (GDPR)

Controller. Ryosuke Igarashi (Softgrove), Japan, is responsible for this policy. Contact: softgrove@duck.com.

Processing and legal bases. DiveLog keeps your app records on your device, and we do not receive or process them. Optional purchases are processed by Apple; see Apple’s Privacy Policy. If you contact us, we process the information you send only to answer your request, based on our legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Recipients, transfers, and retention. The app does not transfer your records to us, Cloudflare, or another developer-operated service. Apple processes purchase information under its own privacy terms. On-device records remain until you delete them or the app. Correspondence is kept only as long as reasonably needed to answer and document the request.

Your rights. Subject to applicable law, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and data portability for information we hold by emailing softgrove@duck.com. Because we do not receive your on-device records, we cannot access or identify them. You may also lodge a complaint with the data-protection supervisory authority in your country of residence or work, or where you believe an infringement occurred.

Providing on-device records to us is not a contractual or statutory requirement. We do not use automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact
